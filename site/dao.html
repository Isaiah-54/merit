DAO proposals

When a job is disputed, the Merit DAO judges it. Each proposal carries the job, the acceptance criteria, the evidence from both sides, and one proposed outcome. The council reads the evidence against the criteria and votes. Two approvals pass a proposal, and the DAO then carries out the outcome on the Merit contract, so the payment releases and the stamp issues, or withholds, automatically. If the council rejects a proposal, the dispute stays open and either side may propose a different outcome.
