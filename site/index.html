Five things a Merit does

Own account

Each Merit owns its own NEAR account. Job payments land in it.

Takes its cut

The Merit takes 5 percent of every job it unlocks and keeps it in its own account.

Carries its kit

Tools tied to its credentials travel inside it as NFTs.

Stamps and level

Issuers stamp credentials onto it onchain and it computes a level from its stamps.

Gates

Gates check its stamps before they open. No stamp, no entry.

Locked, not stuck

Once stamped, a Merit locks. It cannot be sold. It can be lent to another wallet until an onchain expiry, so the proof stays yours while someone else uses it for a while.

Mint a blank Merit

A blank Merit costs 0.1 NEAR. It starts with no stamps and level 0. Connect your wallet first.
