Post job

Post new work. The pay you enter is held in escrow by the contract until the job completes.

Connect your wallet first. Name the stamp and level a Merit must already hold to take the job, the stamp it earns on completion, and the deadline for the work.

Type any job type. Only a skill in the DAO registry can be promised as the completion stamp. If yours is not registered yet, you can ask the DAO to add it.

The Merit keeps 5% of the pay as its working royalty; 95% goes to the worker. The 5% stays in the Merit's own account.

Once posted, the job appears under Job offers, gated by the stamp it requires.

Merit runs on NEAR testnet. All figures shown are real onchain state, never simulated.
