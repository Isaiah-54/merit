Badges

Every badge a Merit can carry, in one place. Each badge is a stamp type recorded onchain: a whitelisted issuer grants it for real work, the level rises with the work, and gated jobs check for it before they open. Below are the badges Merits already hold, and the badges that can still be earned. Each one comes from completing an open job.

A badge's design comes from its level, never its skill: level 1 is a bronze seal with a single chevron, level 2 a silver seal with a double chevron, level 3 a gold seal with a star. Welding 1 and coding 1 share one look; welding 3 and coding 3 share another. The skill is the name written on the badge, and the level is what the badge looks like.
